Featured Functional Case Study

Automating Multi-Tier Sales Approvals & Credit Controls for Regional Distribution

FUNCTIONAL ARCHITECTURE
report_problem The Business Problem

In high-volume regional distribution, sales orders frequently faced dispatch bottlenecks and bad-debt risks due to manual checks of customer credit limits and delayed verification of bank deposit receipts.

psychology My Functional Decision

Designed a mobile staging workflow where orders placed in the field automatically check live Business Central customer credit balances and require verified bank deposit attachments before proceeding through a 4-tier management approval hierarchy.

task_alt The Business Result

Orders are automatically placed on hold if customer credit limits are exceeded or deposit slips are unverified. This eliminated unapproved credit overruns while accelerating approved order handoffs directly into Business Central for fulfillment.
